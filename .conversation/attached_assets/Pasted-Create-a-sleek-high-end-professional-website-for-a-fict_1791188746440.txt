Create a **sleek, high-end, professional website** for a fictional UAE financial consultancy called **NOVA Wealth Advisory**.

This is a portfolio/demo website inspired by a financial consultancy redesign project. The website must look like a **real premium professional-services brand**, not a generic AI-generated template.

The overall design direction is:

**Luxury consulting firm + modern wealth advisory + editorial corporate design**

Think:

* Sophisticated
* Minimal
* Quiet luxury
* Strong typography
* Excellent whitespace
* Precise alignment
* Elegant imagery
* Restrained animation
* Premium but approachable

Do NOT make it flashy, salesy, overly corporate, or visually crowded.

---

# BRAND

**NOVA Wealth Advisory**

**Tagline:**
Clarity for today. Confidence for tomorrow.

**Location:**
Dubai, United Arab Emirates

**Industry:**
Financial planning and wealth advisory

The brand should communicate:

**Trust + Intelligence + Stability + Personal Attention**

---

# VISUAL DIRECTION

Use a very refined visual system.

### Primary colors

* Deep midnight navy: `#0D1724`
* Warm ivory: `#F7F5F0`
* Soft stone: `#E9E5DC`
* Charcoal: `#24272B`
* Muted champagne accent: `#B6A47A`
* White: `#FFFFFF`

Use the champagne color **very sparingly**.

The website should NOT look gold-colored.

The majority of the visual impact should come from:

* typography
* spacing
* photography
* composition
* subtle contrast

---

# TYPOGRAPHY

Use:

**Headings:** Manrope / Neue Haas Grotesk-style / Plus Jakarta Sans
**Body:** Inter

Headings should be:

* Large
* Elegant
* Slightly tight
* High contrast
* Editorial

Example:

**Financial clarity for the life you're building.**

Use large typography rather than decorative graphics.

Avoid excessive bold text.

---

# LAYOUT PRINCIPLES

The design must use:

* Large whitespace
* Wide content containers
* Asymmetrical editorial layouts
* Large photography
* Thin borders
* Small uppercase labels
* Minimal icons
* Strong typographic hierarchy
* Clean grids
* Large section spacing

Avoid:

* Excessive rounded cards
* Huge shadows
* Glassmorphism
* Neon gradients
* Floating blobs
* Decorative circles everywhere
* Excessive icons
* Dashboard-like UI

Cards should generally have:

* Minimal radius or almost square corners
* Thin borders
* Subtle hover transitions

# ANIMATIONS

Keep animations extremely sophisticated.

Use:

* Gentle fade-up reveals
* Smooth text transitions
* Subtle image scaling
* Underline animation
* Arrow movement
* Navbar transformation
* Smooth page transitions

Animation duration around 300–700ms.

Nothing should bounce, spin, or feel playful.

The design should feel **slow, confident and intentional**.

# CONTENT STYLE

Write content like a real premium advisory firm.

Tone:

**Calm. Intelligent. Human. Confident.**

Avoid marketing clichés such as:

"Unlock your financial potential"

"Transform your financial future"

"Take control of your wealth"

"Empowering your financial journey"

Instead use simple, credible language.

Do not create:

* Fake awards
* Fake client numbers
* Fake performance results
* Guaranteed returns
* Fake testimonials
* Fake licenses
* Fake regulatory claims

All information is fictional demo content.

# FINAL QUALITY CHECK

Before finishing, review the entire website.

Remove anything that looks:

* Generic
* Template-based
* Over-designed
* AI-generated
* Too colorful
* Too rounded
* Too sales-oriented

The final result should resemble the website of a **high-end Dubai consulting or wealth advisory firm**.

The most important qualities are:

**Typography > spacing > composition > photography > subtle interaction**

Not:
**gradients > cards > icons > effects**

Make the homepage exceptional enough that it can immediately be used as a **portfolio demo for a real Upwork client seeking a financial consultancy website redesign**.
